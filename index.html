04/ Studio About us

Small team.
Big explosions.

Oh No Games is a small independent studio. We love strategy games, ridiculous ideas and the moment a plan goes horribly wrong — that moment you say “oh no”.

Our games are built in Unity with one rule above all others: more units on screen. We obsess over performance so your battles can have thousands of fighters instead of dozens.

We're currently working on our first title, D.J.T, a satirical mass-battle game. Want to follow along, write about us or work together? Get in touch →
