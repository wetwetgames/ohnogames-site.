02/ The game

When election
meets extinction.

D.J.T is a mass-battle strategy satire. Pick your troops, place your army on the battlefield, press start — and watch hundreds, then thousands of units crash into each other.

Zombies in red caps, raptors, tanks, samurai, geishas, battleships and a T-Rex or two. Grab oil, win votes and climb through a campaign of increasingly ridiculous battles. Strategy, with love and friendship. Mostly kaboom.

03/ FeaturesWhat's inside
01

Thousands
on screen

Built from the ground up for huge battles: 2,000+ units fighting at once, each with its own target, path and attack — dinosaurs, infantry, vehicles and fliers together.

02

Place, press,
watch

Spend your coins, drop your troops inside the deploy zone, hit Start Battle and see if your plan survives contact with a herd of brachiosaurs.

03

25+ wild
troop types

Zombies, HOT agents, rangers, samurai, ninjas, geishas, gold monks, pandas, raptors, triceratops, T-Rex, megalodon, helicopters, jets, tanks, missile trucks and battleships. Win stages to unlock more.

04

A campaign
of chaos

30 stages across deserts, wildlands, forests, snow and mud, with story cutscenes and a cast of very fictional world leaders who are all definitely not who you think they are.
